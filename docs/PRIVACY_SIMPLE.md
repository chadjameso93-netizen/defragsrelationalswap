# DEFRAG privacy in simple language

## What DEFRAG stores
DEFRAG may store:
- your account information
- your baseline profile details
- your saved sessions and workspace history
- your billing state

## What DEFRAG should not store unless needed
DEFRAG should avoid storing:
- more personal relationship detail than necessary
- unnecessary copies of the same conversation
- secrets or credentials in logs

## What may be sent to AI systems
DEFRAG may send selected parts of your input to AI systems in order to generate guidance.

DEFRAG should only send:
- the minimum context needed to answer well
- the parts of your session that matter for the current request

## What DEFRAG should avoid sending
DEFRAG should avoid sending:
- unnecessary account details
- unnecessary personal identifiers
- unrelated session history

## What users should be able to do
Users should be able to:
- understand what is stored
- understand what is sent to AI
- delete their account
- delete saved sessions if the product stores them
- export their data if needed

## How DEFRAG should describe itself
DEFRAG should explain clearly that it is:
- a relational intelligence product
- not a therapist
- not a diagnosis tool
- not a crisis service

## Product rule
If a privacy or trust explanation is too complex for a normal user to understand quickly, rewrite it until it is simple.
